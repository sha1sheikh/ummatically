What We Teach

Horse Riding. Archery. Mounted Archery.

Three disciplines from our tradition, taught on our own farm in Worcestershire. Few places in the country teach the third at all.

Horse Riding

Learn horsemanship from the ground up — handling, riding, and building real trust with the animal.

Archery

Traditional bow work: form, breath and focus. A Sunnah practice that rewards patience over strength.

Mounted Archery

The two brought together — and the rarest of the three. Our academy takes you from novice rider to mounted archer.

Our Story

Our Story

A Mission for the Revival of Our Ummah

With over ten years of community support work helping people through the challenges they face, Abu Obaida founded Muslims of Ihsan with a simple mission: to help Muslims re-establish their faith and rediscover the strength that comes from living by it.

We believe a better future is possible for our Ummah — and that every Muslim is a building block in the wall we hope to see built.

During these deeply troubled times, witnessing the immense suffering of our people strengthened our resolve to contribute in whatever way we could. We opened our farm and began developing an activity centre with the hope of making this vision of Ummah revival a reality.

Through that journey, we have refined our methods and developed our capabilities to offer something unique to Muslims in our region — a space for strengthening, purification, calm, resilience and support.

Our hope is that those who come here leave with a renewed sense of connection: to our Ummah, to our Prophet ﷺ, and ultimately to Allah سبحانه وتعالى.

May Allah keep us sincere and allow us to remain in service to Him. Ameen.
